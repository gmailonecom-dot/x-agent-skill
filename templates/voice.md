# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/x/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in this pack reads it. Ten minutes
here is worth more than any prompt you will ever write, because on X your voice is the only thing a
stranger has to judge you by before they follow.

## Who I am talking to

One person. Name them properly — not "founders", but "a solo developer shipping their first product
who is tired of posting into the void".

## How I actually talk

Paste five of your own posts that did well. Not what you wish you sounded like.

## Words I never use

The ones that are not mine. Be specific: "unlock", "game-changer", "let's dive in", "hot take".

## Words I do use

Your tics. The phrases that are yours. Keep them.

## What I will not claim

Numbers I cannot show, results that are not mine, tools I have not used. Write them down so the
skills refuse them for you instead of you catching it after you post.

## My format

Length, how a post opens, whether you use line breaks, whether you use emoji, whether you thread or
post singles, whether you reply to your own replies.
